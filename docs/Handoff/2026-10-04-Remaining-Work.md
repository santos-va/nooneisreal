## What was not silently abandoned

The city/NPC, anatomy, item readability, final audio/voices and workflow tracks remain explicit above. They were not completed merely because newer requests arrived. Existing plans retain historical details and may still say `approved` rather than `done`; that can mean player acceptance is outstanding even after implementation merged. Conversely, an asset or planning PR can be merged with no corresponding gameplay integration.

Early failed native fixtures and rejected visual captures are diagnostic history, not acceptance. Historical old-PR audit debt (including #144–#155) must be checked individually if that audit track resumes; later T4 reports do not retroactively clear every earlier PR.
