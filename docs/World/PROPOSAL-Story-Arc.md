## 1. Canon baseline — what Santos already said [C]

Everything below is a short English rendering of canon. If a line here disagrees with [[Lore]], **[[Lore]] wins**.

- Choko leads a team of heroes; the city is Cronshift (still spelled [[Kronshift]] in the wiki until #12). Choko's
  watch stops time; his glyphs "record" strikes. — [[Lore]] § Що відомо
- Choko and Skea are in **one squad**, very close comrades who went through a lot together. The squad's name, purpose
  and other members are **in development**. — [[2026-10-03-Choko-Skea-Bond-and-Curse]], [[2026-10-03-Skea-Curse-Lore-Answers]]
- **The curse.** Skea was in the **catacombs of an old building in the city**; a **spirit seized him** there. Choko was
  nearby but **outside, in the street**. Later Choko **got into the building, where Skea was left unconscious**.
- **The one who cursed** (name unknown) **feeds on Skea's spirit** — that is why Skea became a psychopath. Before, Skea
  was **friendly and honest with everyone**; now he says almost nothing about himself.
- **The curser's aim:** to **lay down every living thing himself**, so that **only he remains, with the shadows of
  those he cursed** — his "harmonious", "peaceful" world with no other mind in it.
- **Why Skea:** he wanted only **freedom to act** and lived by one law — **"bring no pain to the world, and the world
  will be good to you."** That is why his soul fit so well. He is **not the only one** — just one of the good people.
- **12 souls, 11 bodies.** The curser **took 12 souls and 11 bodies**. They tried to take Skea too and **failed, for
  an unknown reason** — Skea was left **"halfway"**.
- **Shaper** — Skea's **shadow**, summoned during the curse; an **enemy** that appeared because Skea was cursed; now
  on the curser's side. **Shaper is one of the 12.** Shaper is **not** Skea's ultimate.
- **Skea's goal:** to **cut down all 12 pages**, above all **Shaper**.
- **One of the 12 "is still theirs, from the team"** — another character can **summon them from an amulet** (Santos
  will tell more later).
- **The ∞8** — "**the ones who want to stop** the curser"; they do everything to find him. The sigil lives only on the
  back panel of Skea's grimoire-backpack: alive, but **no eyes, teeth or mouth, no pulse**; moving, it leaves a **neon
  trail that disperses**. The grimoire is a cursed book. — [[Skea]] § Рюкзак-гримуар і ∞8
- Names and effects of the 12 — **later, from Santos**.

## 2. Logline [P]

*In Cronshift, a man who believes peace means silence is harvesting good souls to build a world where only he is
real. He took twelve. The twelfth — Skea — he only got halfway. Now Skea walks with half a soul, a grin that isn't his,
and a book of eight wardens on his back that will not stop pointing at the man who did it. Choko, who pulled him out
of the catacombs, is the only one who remembers who Skea used to be.*

## 3. Holes and how Klio proposes to close them

Each hole has an ID. Answer by ID: **"H3 — так"**, **"H3 — ні"**, or **"H3 — інакше: …"**.

### H1. Why did the taking stop "halfway"? [P]

Canon only says "for an unknown reason". Three options, Klio recommends **A**:

- **A — Choko's time (recommended).** The taking is not instant: the curser draws the soul first and the body last.
  When Choko forced his way into the building, his watch reacted on its own and **stopped time in the catacombs for a
  heartbeat**. The thread between Skea and the curser snapped mid-pull — half the soul gone, the body left behind.
  **Choko does not know he did it.** It stays "unknown" in the story until Act III, when the ∞8 shows him.
  *Why it fits:* uses Choko's canon power, explains why he could get in only after Skea fell, and gives the duo a
  hidden bond that pays off in the finale.
- **B — Skea's law.** A soul that never caused pain cannot be fully taken; the curse tore on his own goodness.
- **C — The ∞8 intervened.** The wardens caught the falling half and bound themselves to Skea as their compass.

### H2. Whose is the twelfth soul? (12 souls, 11 bodies) [P] [?]

Canon: 12 souls, 11 bodies, Skea halfway, Shaper is Skea's shadow and one of the 12. Klio's **proposed reading**
(Santos did **not** say this):

- 11 people lost **soul and body** → 11 shadow-pages with stolen bodies to walk in.
- The 12th soul is **Skea's taken half** → it became **Shaper**: a shadow **without a body of his own**.
- **Shaper wants to finish the job** — to take Skea's body and become whole. Skea wants to kill Shaper.
  **The dilemma:** if Skea cuts Shaper down, does his half die with it — or come home?

**[?] Q1** — Is the 12th soul Skea's half (and Shaper made of it)? Or is Skea's soul outside the 12?

### H3. Who are the ∞8? [P] [?]

Canon: "the ones who want to stop him", living, faceless, pulseless, a trail that disperses. Klio proposes:

- **The Eight** are eight people who **stopped the curser once before**, long ago, and sealed themselves into a book
  so they could keep watching for him. **∞** = while the book exists they cannot finish dying.
- They chose Skea because a **half-taken soul is still tied to the curser** — Skea is the only living compass that
  points at him. The **neon trail** is the Eight "sniffing" that tie; in gameplay it can lean toward the next story
  target.
- That is also why the grimoire **looks** cursed while being on the side of good: it is made of the dead.

**[?] Q2** — Is "∞8" one being or eight ("ті, що хочуть зупинити" reads as many)? Are they people who were once alive?

### H4. Why does Skea grin, and why does he say nothing about himself? [P]

Canon: the curser feeds on Skea's spirit → psychopath; the supernatural smile, eyes not smiling.

- **The smile is not Skea's.** It leaks through the feeding link — the curser's (or Shaper's) satisfaction showing on
  Skea's face. **When the curser feeds, the smile widens**; the faint violet glow behind the teeth from
  [[2026-10-03-Skea-Redesign]] becomes the visual tell.
- **He says nothing about himself because he cannot tell which thoughts are his.** Silence is the last honest thing
  the old Skea can still do — a quiet echo of his law.
- **Shaper has the same smile** — and his eyes *do* smile. That is how players learn to tell them apart.

### H5. Skea's ultimate vs. the 12 — a contradiction to fix [P] [?]

The kit ([[Skea]] § Кіт, [[03-Skills-Framework]]) says the ult **"Cursed Grimoire ∞8: 12 Seals"** opens the book and
**three sealed villains strike**, with pages named like **"Page 12 «Shadow»"**. But in canon the 12 are **enemies on
the curser's side**, and Shaper is **not** Skea's ult. As written, Skea summons his own enemies.

- **Proposal:** the grimoire holds **imprints**, not the 12 themselves. **Every page Skea cuts down leaves its imprint
  in the book**, and the Eight let him throw that imprint as an attack. Story progress = more pages in the ult
  (variant A of the ult — choose 3 of the cut pages — becomes a natural unlock).
- **Rename** "Page 12 «Shadow»" in the kit so nobody reads it as Shaper.

**[?] Q3** — Accept "imprints" as the frame for the ult? (If yes → T5 Ares owns the kit wording, not Klio.)

### H6. The curser [P] [?]

Canon: name unknown; feeds on Skea; wants to lay down all life and stay alone with the shadows.

- **Working codename (not a name): "the Quiet One".** Placeholder only, for docs and prompts, until Santos names him.
- **Motive:** he is the **mirror of Skea's law**. Skea: *bring no pain and the world will be good to you.* The Quiet
  One: *other minds are where pain comes from — remove them and nothing will hurt.* He harvests **good** souls because
  they are the only ones calm enough to become obedient shadows. That is the deep reason Skea's soul "fit".
- He is human, or was; he took the name of peace and means emptiness.

**[?] Q4** — Is he human? Does he have a face/look yet? Keep the codename or leave him nameless in docs?

### H7. The squad [P] [?]

Canon: in development. Proposal only, nothing assumed:

- **Purpose:** an unofficial crew that looks into **disappearances in Cronshift** — the 11 missing bodies are how they
  find the curse in the first place. Tess's card passive "repairs broken evidence" ([[Roster]]) already fits a crew that
  investigates.
- **Name ideas** (pick, mix or throw away): **Late Shift** (a pun on Cronshift and night work) · **Second Hand** (a
  clock hand, and "help that comes after") · **The Watch**.
- **Members** — Choko (leader), Skea; the rest from [[Roster]] when Santos decides.

### H8. The amulet character and the teammate among the 12 [P] [?]

Canon: one of the 12 "is still theirs, from the team"; another character summons them from an amulet; Santos will tell
more later. Klio does **not** pick the character, only notes a fit to check:

- [[Roster]] lists **Sneak** — a bald healer **with a yellow amulet** (from his card prompts). If that is the amulet,
  the healer who could not save a teammate keeps the last of them in it — and can call their shadow back, for a moment,
  against the other 11.

**[?] Q5** — Is the amulet character Sneak, or someone else? Is the teammate among the 12 a separate person (not Skea)?

### H9. The old building and the catacombs [P]

- **The old clock tower** (canon city references show a clock tower over Cronshift — [[Kronshift]]). Catacombs under
  it. It ties Choko's watch, the city's name (*cron* — time) and the "stopped halfway" moment (H1-A) into one place.
- Alternatives: an old archive, a shut-down station.

### H10. What "No One Is Real" means (interview F1) [P]

- **In the curser's finished world, no one is real** — only shadows wearing the faces of the taken, and him.
- **Before that,** shadows can wear the faces of people you know. Heroes stop trusting what they see — no one is real.
- **For Skea,** half of him is gone; he is not sure he is real either.

### H11. Why do the heroes fight each other (interview F2)? [P]

- **Echoes.** The 12 and lesser shadows can **wear the shape of anyone whose shadow they touched**. Most versus fights
  are against an echo of a teammate — that is the story frame for mirror matches and hero-vs-hero.
- **Arcade frame:** *"Echo Trial"* — the Eight test the squad against echoes so they can tell shadow from friend.
- Fits [[Roster]]: Mirage's "ghost double" and hand mirror.

## 4. Proposed arc [P]

Story mode is out of scope for 0.x ([[01-Vision]] § Чого не робимо в 0.x) — this is a frame for later, and for arcade
intros/endings now. Stages are the ones that already exist or are planned on [[Kronshift]].

| part | beat | playable fight | stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prologue — The Catacombs** | Night. Choko waits in the street; something goes wrong below; he forces his way in and finds Skea unconscious. (Canon up to here.) | Tutorial: Choko vs. an echo in the dark | back alley (night) |
| **Act I — Half** | Skea wakes up wrong: the grin, the silence. The squad looks into the disappearances; the first stolen body walks the market. | Choko vs. Skea — Skea not himself (the first "is it really him?" fight) · Skea vs. a page | market street, bazaar |
| **Act II — Pages** | The ∞8 trail leads from page to page. Skea cuts one down; its imprint enters the book (H5). The amulet calls back the teammate's shadow for one fight. **Shaper appears** — Skea's face, Skea's smile, eyes that smile. | Skea vs. page · Choko vs. Shaper (wearing Skea) | main street, river |
| **Act III — No One Is Real** | The Quiet One begins: streets empty, people turn to shadows. The Eight show Choko what his watch did in the catacombs (H1-A). | Skea vs. Shaper | the clock tower (H9) |
| **Finale** | Choko and Skea vs. the Quiet One under the clock. | duo / tag fight (future mode) | fountain square at night, CRONSHIFT neon |

**Endings for Santos to choose** [?] **Q6**:

- **A — Whole.** Cutting Shaper returns Skea's half; the smile fades for the first time. The other 11 souls go free.
- **B — The price.** Choko uses RECORD on Skea's soul — rewinding him to the moment before the catacombs — and loses
  something of his own (time, memory, the watch).
- **C — Open.** Skea wins but keeps the smile. Nobody, Skea included, knows which half came back.
